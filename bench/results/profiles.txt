Sampling profiles of the LRL front half (context for experiments 1 and 5; not timing measurements)
=================================================================================================

Taken 2026-10-08 after all timing batches had finished, with the same release CLI
(sha256 e50af7a629ec5801f3d0ffb4fa18b589476f6ccfcecd226861119e2fad349820) and the no-op rustc of
the driver first on PATH, from the repository root in a shell with `ulimit -s 65520`. macOS
`/usr/bin/sample` (default 1 ms interval) attached to the running `lrl compile`. The raw `sample`
reports are not kept in the repository (14766618 and 459507269 bytes of text); the counts below
were extracted with `bench/sample_summary.py <report> <frame>...`, which prints, for each named
frame, the samples of its outermost occurrences and their split over its direct callees.
Counts are samples of the compiler thread (`Thread_...: lrl`); the main thread only waits in
`pthread_join`.

(a) case_studies/lrl/vectors.lrl, --backend typed, front half (experiment 1, `cli_front_noop_rustc`)
    PATH="bench/work/compile/shim:$PATH" <cli> compile case_studies/lrl/vectors.lrl --backend typed -o <bin> &
    /usr/bin/sample $! 6 -file vectors_typed_front.sample.txt
    The report covers the first 6 s of the run (Launch Time 01:35:11.250, Date/Time 01:35:11.316);
    the median of this front half is 9.988 s (results/summary.md).
    Compiler thread: 4459 samples; 4334 under cli::run_cli -> cli::compiler::compile_with_mir (the
    other 125 have an incomplete recorded stack).
      cli::compiler::compile_with_mir                     4334
        cli::driver::process_code(_inner)                 4323
          cli::driver::validate_definition_mir            3257  (3246 of them in mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term;
                                                                 1968 of these via mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_erased_proof_value)
          kernel::checker::Env::add_definition             394
          kernel::checker::check                           338
          frontend::elaborator::Elaborator::check          318
      mir::lower::LoweringContext::infer_term_type        3133  (all in kernel::checker::infer)
      kernel::checker::whnf_in_ctx                        3164  (2448 in kernel::nbe::normalize_for_typing,
                                                                 312 in kernel::checker::eval_env_from_ctx,
                                                                 259 in drop_in_place<kernel::nbe::Value>)
      kernel::nbe::normalize_for_typing (all callers)     2450  (1491 in kernel::nbe::quote_with_config,
                                                                 157 in kernel::nbe::eval_with_config)
    i.e. 3133 / 4334 = 72% of the sampled front-half time is type inference called from MIR
    lowering, and 3164 / 4334 = 73% is spent under the kernel's weak-head normaliser (from any caller).

(b) bench/work/indexcost/vec_build_sum_closed_index_n1000.lrl (experiment 5, closed size expression
    in a type index, n = 1000; median of this front half 16.898 s in results/summary.md)
    PATH="bench/work/compile/shim:$PATH" <cli> compile <that file> --backend typed -o <bin> &
    (wait 5 s) /usr/bin/sample $! 10 -file ic1000_typed_front.sample.txt
    The report covers 5-15 s into the run (Launch Time 01:36:33.946, Date/Time 01:36:39.005).
    Compiler thread: 5388 samples. The stacks are so deep that `sample` recorded the full stack
    (down to thread_start) for only 282 of them. Outermost recorded frame of the other samples:
      kernel::nbe::* (apply_with_config 803, apply_recursor_with_args 793, try_reduce_rec 767,
                      drop_in_place<kernel::nbe::Value> 576, <kernel::nbe::Value as Clone>::clone 527,
                      check_eq_vec 15, eval_with_config 10, check_eq 8, normalize_for_typing 1,
                      extend_env 1)                                                              3501
      <Vec<T> as Clone>::clone (its caller is not recorded)                                       1572
      other                                                                                        33
    All 288 samples whose recorded stack reaches cli::driver::process_code_inner (the 282 complete
    ones and 6 partial ones) split into 166 under kernel::checker::Env::add_definition (the kernel's
    check of a definition; via its closure: 107 in kernel::checker::check, 60 in
    kernel::checker::check_ownership_in_term_with_capture) and 122 under
    cli::driver::validate_definition_mir -> mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term (MIR lowering;
    direct callees mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term_to_local 98 and
    kernel::checker::whnf_in_ctx 24; mir::lower::LoweringContext::infer_term_type 25 samples, all in
    kernel::checker::infer). 260 samples are under kernel::checker::whnf_in_ctx, all via
    kernel::nbe::normalize_for_typing -> kernel::nbe::eval_with_config: 132 of them under
    kernel::checker::Env::add_definition, 122 under mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term (i.e. all
    of the MIR-lowering samples), 6 in partial stacks reaching neither. Of the samples under
    kernel::nbe::eval_with_config (outermost occurrences), 162 are under
    kernel::checker::Env::add_definition, 122 under mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term and 1238
    in partial stacks reaching neither (counted with a helper script, kept outside the repository, that
    checks the recorded ancestors of each outermost occurrence).
    Most samples (3501 of 5388) have a frame of the kernel's evaluator (kernel::nbe) as outermost
    recorded frame; the samples whose stack reaches the driver reach the evaluator both from the
    kernel's definition check and from MIR lowering. Because only 288 of 5388 stacks reach the
    driver, the profile does not apportion the time between the two.
