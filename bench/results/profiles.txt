Sampling profiles of the LRL front half (context for experiments 1 and 5; not timing measurements)
=================================================================================================

Taken 2026-10-05 after all timing batches had finished, with the same release CLI
(sha256 43640e57f8be283a369d266b2c2d2f0d325be3f69d53911d4de39c1a51f1da13) and the no-op rustc of
the driver first on PATH. macOS `/usr/bin/sample` (default 1 ms interval) attached to the running
`lrl compile`. The raw `sample` reports are not kept in the repository (14 MB and 474 MB of text);
the counts below were extracted with `bench/sample_summary.py <report> <frame>...`, which prints, for
each named frame, the samples of its outermost occurrences and their split over its direct callees.
Counts are samples of the compiler thread (`Thread_...: lrl`); the main thread only waits in
`pthread_join`.

(a) case_studies/lrl/vectors.lrl, --backend typed, front half (experiment 1, `cli_front_noop_rustc`)
    PATH="bench/work/compile/shim:$PATH" <cli> compile case_studies/lrl/vectors.lrl --backend typed -o <bin> &
    /usr/bin/sample $! 6 -file vectors_typed_front.sample.txt
    The report covers the first 6 s of the run (Launch Time 19:52:18.600, Date/Time 19:52:18.657).
    Compiler thread: 4890 samples; 4849 under cli::run_cli -> cli::compiler::compile_with_mir.
      cli::compiler::compile_with_mir                     4849
        cli::driver::process_code(_inner)                 3992
          cli::driver::validate_definition_mir            2799  (2774 of them in mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term)
          kernel::checker::Env::add_definition             451
          kernel::checker::check                           373
          frontend::elaborator::Elaborator::check          354
        mir::lower::LoweringContext::lower_term            826
      mir::lower::LoweringContext::infer_term_type        3003  (all in kernel::checker::infer)
      kernel::checker::whnf_in_ctx                        3662  (2444 in kernel::nbe::quote_with_fuel,
                                                                 406 in kernel::nbe::eval_with_fuel)
    i.e. 3003 / 4849 = 62% of the sampled front-half time is type inference called from MIR
    lowering, and 3662 / 4849 = 76% is spent under the kernel's weak-head normaliser (from any caller).

(b) bench/work/indexcost/vec_build_sum_closed_index_n1000.lrl (experiment 5, closed size expression
    in a type index, n = 1000; this front half takes about 39 s)
    PATH="bench/work/compile/shim:$PATH" <cli> compile <that file> --backend typed -o <bin> &
    (wait 15 s) /usr/bin/sample $! 10 -file ic1000_typed_front.sample.txt
    The report covers 15-25 s into the run (Launch Time 19:54:24.149, Date/Time 19:54:39.252).
    Compiler thread: 5553 samples. The stacks are so deep that `sample` recorded the full stack
    (down to thread_start) for only 294 of them. Outermost recorded frame of the other samples:
      kernel::nbe::* (apply_with_config, apply_recursor_with_args, try_reduce_rec,
                      drop_in_place<kernel::nbe::Value>, <kernel::nbe::Value as Clone>::clone)  3593
      <Vec<T> as Clone>::clone (called from <kernel::nbe::Value as Clone>::clone)                1624
      other                                                                                        42
    All 304 samples whose recorded stack reaches cli::driver::process_code_inner (the 294 complete
    ones and 10 partial ones) are under kernel::checker::Env::add_definition (the kernel's check of a
    definition); 225 samples are under kernel::checker::whnf_in_ctx (224 of them in
    kernel::nbe::eval_with_fuel). No sample reaches mir::lower in this window.
    An exploratory sample of the same program shape at n = 8000 (same binary, taken about 10 min
    into that run, before the timing run; kept outside the repository) showed the time under
    mir::lower::LoweringContext::infer_term_type -> kernel::checker::infer ->
    kernel::checker::whnf_in_ctx -> kernel::nbe. Both profiles place the cost in the kernel's
    evaluator (kernel::nbe) normalising the closed size expression; one reaches it from the kernel's
    definition check, the other from type inference during MIR lowering.
